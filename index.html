What we do

What is Fractional Legal Support?

Fractional legal support (often called fractional general counsel) gives you experienced, strategic counsel on a part-time or project basis. You get the benefit of in-house legal leadership without the cost or commitment of a full-time hire. It's a practical fit for startups and growth-stage companies, as well as established teams running lean.

JPG Law provides flexible, business-forward support that scales with your needs, whether that's day-to-day issue spotting, a financing push, or ongoing contract and compliance coverage. Our work commonly includes multi-state employment compliance, privacy and information governance, commercial transactions, corporate governance, and risk management, delivered with continuity and context so legal becomes a strategic function, not a fire drill.
