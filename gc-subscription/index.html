What Is a GC Subscription?

A real working relationship. Not a ticket queue.

Most companies don't need a full-time general counsel — but they do need someone who knows their contracts, understands their industry, and is reachable before a problem turns into a crisis.

A GC Subscription with JPG Law gives you exactly that. Ongoing legal coverage at a flat monthly rate. We handle your contracts, compliance questions, employment matters, and day-to-day legal needs as a continuous part of your operations — not as a series of one-off engagements.

You get direct access to Jason. Not a paralegal, not a case management system, not someone who needs to get up to speed every time you call. When something comes up, you reach the attorney who already knows your business.

Pacta contract intelligence is included for all GC Subscription clients — at no additional cost. Learn more below.

Who It's Built For

Legal coverage matched to how you actually operate

The GC Subscription is designed for companies with ongoing, structural legal needs — not occasional one-off questions. If your business regularly touches contracts, employment, data, or compliance, you're leaving value on the table with hourly outside counsel.

💻
IT & Technology Staffing Firms

You're placing software engineers, IT consultants, and technical project managers against demanding client MSAs. Your contracts have indemnification teeth, your workers face classification pressure, and you're operating across multiple states. You need a GC who has been in that room — not one who's reading the staffing industry playbook for the first time.

☁️
SaaS & Technology Companies

You're negotiating vendor agreements, customer MSAs, and data processing addendums on a rolling basis. Privacy obligations are real — GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA adjacency — and they touch your product roadmap, not just your paper. A GC Subscription means those issues are handled continuously, not after they've become a blocker.

🌍
Employers Scaling Nationally

Multi-state employment is complicated by design. Wage and hour, non-compete enforceability, leave law patchwork, independent contractor rules — every new state adds a layer. If you're expanding headcount or entering new markets, ongoing counsel is far cheaper than cleaning up what gets missed.

Coverage

What's included

The GC Subscription covers the full range of legal needs that come with running a technology-forward business. The goal is a comprehensive relationship — not a menu of billable line items.

📄
Contract Review & Negotiation

MSAs, SOWs, vendor and customer agreements, NDAs, licensing deals, and SaaS terms. Redlines, issue spotting, and negotiation support — not just a sign-off.

👥
Employment Law Guidance

Worker classification, multi-state compliance, handbook and policy review, hiring and termination, non-competes, and day-to-day HR legal questions.

🔒
Privacy & Data Protection

GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and state-level privacy law compliance. Data processing agreements, privacy policies, vendor data reviews, and breach response planning. CIPP/US certified.

✅
Compliance & Risk Management

Ongoing compliance monitoring, multi-state regulatory tracking, insurance requirement review, and proactive risk flagging before issues surface.

🏭
Corporate Governance

Board-level guidance, entity structure, operating agreements, equity documentation, and governance frameworks for companies with active leadership teams.

💬
Day-to-Day Legal Access

Questions that don't fit a category. Pre-call sanity checks, quick issue reviews, and the kind of real-time guidance that prevents small decisions from becoming large problems.

What's not included

Litigation and active court representation, government investigations, and matters requiring licensure in jurisdictions where Jason is not admitted. When a matter calls for specialized or local counsel, Jason coordinates the engagement and remains involved as your legal point of contact — so you're never navigating outside counsel alone.
