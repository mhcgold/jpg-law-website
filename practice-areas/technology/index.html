Technology & SaaS Transactions

Technology & SaaS Transactions

Liability-first dealmaking for software, platforms, and data

Technology and SaaS transaction support

We help product, sales, legal, and procurement teams move from "great demo" to signed—fast, defensible, and aligned to how the product actually works. The goal is simple: ship deals without hidden risk, stalled implementations, or security/privacy surprises.

Close-Ready Contracts

MSA/SOW, order forms, subscriptions, reseller/partner terms, marketplace terms, and procurement addenda—structured to close cleanly and scale.

Service Levels That Make Sense

SLA language that matches real operations: support tiers, maintenance windows, DR/BCP, RTO/RPO, and service credits that are fair and administrable.

Data, Privacy, & Security

DPA strategy, cross-border data approaches, right-sized controls, audit rights, pen-test language, and incident response alignment. Healthcare? HIPAA/BAA-ready.

IP & Open Source

IP ownership, work-for-hire, output rights, OSS disclosures, and third-party flow-downs—cleanly allocated to who controls the risk.

Who we support

  • SaaS and tech companies selling subscription software, APIs, or data-driven products
  • Buyers of enterprise software (security/privacy/procurement/legal stakeholders)
  • Founders, product leaders, and GTM teams who need scalable contract paper and faster deal cycles
  • HR tech, workforce-tech, and platform businesses operating in regulated environments
  • AI-enabled vendors and companies licensing AI/ML capabilities (training, outputs, safety)
  • Companies building vendor management programs (risk, security, privacy, and procurement alignment)
  • Teams running high-velocity contracting (Deal Desk needs, redlines, fallback positions)

Tools we provide

  • Clause library (MSA/SOW/SLA/DPA) + fallback positions
  • Pricing & renewal riders (usage tiers, true-ups, uplifts, auto-renew)
  • AI/ML term sheet (training/output options, safety controls)
  • Negotiation playbook for sales and procurement (what we can/can't agree to)

Engagement options

  • Deal Desk: Pre-screen, redline, negotiate, and close in-flow
  • Paper Refresh Sprint (30–45 days): Upgrade templates, playbooks, and guardrails
  • Enterprise Buyer Support: Vendor selection support + security/privacy alignment + seller-paper redlines
  • On-Call Negotiation: Rapid help for high-value or red-flag deals

Outcomes matter

Faster cycles. Fewer escalations. Contracts that match the product—and risk positions leadership can live with.
