Commercial Contracts & Negotiation

Contract work that moves at the speed of business

Efficient, deal-minded drafting and redlining that protects your position while keeping negotiations moving and relationships intact.

What we do

  • Agreement drafting from scratch or from client templates
  • Redline review and negotiation of counterparty paper
  • Master service agreements (MSAs) and related SOWs/orders
  • Non-disclosure and confidentiality agreements (NDAs)
  • Vendor and supplier contracts
  • Customer and client agreements
  • Licensing agreements
  • Letter of intent (LOI) and memorandum of understanding (MOU) drafting
  • Contract playbook development for sales and procurement teams
  • Standard form review and template builds
How clients engage us

Commercial contract clients often engage us on a fractional basis to handle all incoming and outgoing contract review, or on a project basis for specific negotiations. We build client-specific playbooks and fallback positions so your team can move faster on routine agreements without waiting for legal every time.
