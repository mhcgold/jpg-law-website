Commercial Contracts & Negotiation

Commercial Contracts & Negotiation

Cleaner deals. Faster closes. Fewer surprises.

Commercial contracts and negotiation support

We help business, sales, procurement, and operations teams get from "terms in an email" to signed—fast, clear, and aligned to how the work actually gets done. Expect practical redlines, predictable positions, and contracts that reduce friction now and risk later.

Contract Drafting & Redlines

MSAs, SOWs, order forms, amendments, and statements of work—built to close and built to hold up (scope, fees, change control, acceptance, and remedies).

Vendor & Supplier Agreements

Vendor and supplier terms that are workable for the business and protective on risk: flow-downs, insurance, security/privacy terms, audit rights, and dispute mechanics.

Negotiating Strategy & Risk Positions

Clear fallback positions on indemnity, limitation of liability, warranty scope, consequential damages, insurance, and escalations are controlled.

Confidentiality, IP, & Commercial Protections

NDAs, confidentiality programs, IP ownership/allocation, work-made-for-hire/assignments where appropriate, non-solicit/poaching clauses, and practical enforcement readiness.

Due Diligence & Contract Cleanup

Contract portfolio reviews, risk flagging, and "fix lists" (assignment/consent, nonstandard indemnities, renewal/termination traps, pricing exposure) with a remediation plan.

M&A + Complex Transactions

Support for acquisitions and larger commercial deals: purchase agreement support (as needed), TSAs, integration planning for contract migration, and closing checklists that keep operations moving.

Who we support

  • Companies that buy or sell services and need clean, repeatable paper
  • Teams dealing with vendor/procurement addenda and constant "paper battles"
  • Leaders who want consistent risk positions across customers and suppliers
  • Operators who need terms that match reality (delivery, invoicing, change control)
  • Growing businesses building a template stack and clause playbook

Tools we provide

  • MSA/SOW clause library + fallback positions (playbooked)
  • Negotiation "deal desk" checklist (what to push, accept, or escalate)
  • Change-order + acceptance templates (to prevent scope drift disputes)
  • Pricing/renewal riders (uplifts, true-ups, auto-renew guardrails)
  • Due diligence contract review checklist + summary risk matrix
  • One-page "terms guide" for sales/procurement (do/don't language)

Engagement options

  • Deal Desk: in-flow redlines, negotiations, and close support
  • Template Refresh Sprint (30–45 days): modernize your paper + playbooks
  • Vendor/Customer Paper Support: tackle addenda and "one-off" terms fast
  • Portfolio Review: diligence/cleanup of your contract stack
  • On-Call Negotiation: rapid help on high-value or red-flag deals
  • Ongoing Counsel: monthly coverage for predictable support

Outcomes matter

Faster cycles, fewer escalations, clearer operational terms, and contracts that match how the business runs—so you're not re-litigating scope and responsibility later.
