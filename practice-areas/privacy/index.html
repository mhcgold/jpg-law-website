Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy counsel built for the way businesses actually handle data

Risk-aware privacy and data protection counsel—policies, contracting, and incident-ready practices that fit your business and evolving legal requirements.

What we do

  • Privacy program development and policy drafting
  • CCPA/CPRA compliance for California-facing businesses
  • GDPR compliance for businesses with EU customers or employees
  • State privacy law compliance: Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, and more
  • Data processing agreements (DPAs) and data transfer mechanisms
  • Privacy notices and consent management
  • Employee and HR data privacy compliance
  • Vendor privacy due diligence and contracting
  • Data breach and incident response planning
  • AI and automated decision-making compliance considerations
How clients engage us

Privacy clients range from technology companies building data-intensive products to employers managing sensitive employee data across multiple states. Jason holds a CIPP/US certification from the IAPP, reflecting depth in U.S. privacy law. We approach privacy as a business function—practical, proportionate, and built to scale.
