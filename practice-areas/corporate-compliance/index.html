Corporate Compliance & Governance

Board-ready governance and compliance that scales with the business

Corporate compliance and governance support

We help founders, executives, and HR/ops leaders turn "we should probably have a policy for that" into a clear, defensible compliance and governance system. The goal is simple: reduce surprises, document decisions, and build controls that teams can actually follow—without slowing the business down.

Governance that Holds Up

Board and leadership structure, authority matrices, resolutions/consents, and decision documentation—so responsibilities are clear and approvals are defensible.

Compliance Programs that Fit

Codes of conduct, reporting/escalation paths, training, and enforcement frameworks built to fit your size and risk profile—not a generic binder.

Workforce Infrastructure

Handbooks, onboarding/offboarding workflows, manager playbooks, and documentation standards—built for a multi-state or regulated workforce.

Investigations & Response Readiness

Intake triage, privileged investigations, evidence preservation, and clean writeups—so internal issues don't become external problems.

Operational Policies

Conflicts, gifts, confidentiality, records retention, privacy/security alignment, and AI use guidelines—written so teams can follow them consistently.

Who we support

  • Founder-led and growth-stage companies formalizing governance and controls
  • Operating leaders who need "real" policies that match how work gets done
  • HR/People teams building consistent documentation, discipline, and reporting workflows
  • Organizations preparing for audits, diligence, or enterprise customer requirements
  • Teams managing third-party/vendor risk (including workforce-tech and AI tools)
  • Businesses operating across multiple states or regulated environments

Tools we provide

  • Governance toolkit (resolutions/consents, delegations of authority, committee charters)
  • Policy suite + manager-ready procedures (not just "policies," but how to run them)
  • Complaint intake + investigation playbook (triage, privilege, documentation templates)
  • Vendor risk pack (diligence checklist, security/privacy addenda, approval workflow)
  • Compliance training outlines + tracking approach sized to your environment
  • One-page field guides for leaders (what gets escalated, when, and to whom)

Engagement options

  • Fractional GC Compliance Partner: Ongoing governance + compliance support in-flow
  • Program Build Sprint (30–45 days): Stand up core policies, workflows, and documentation standards
  • Governance Refresh: Clean up entity/board materials, authorities, and record keeping
  • Hotline/Investigation Support: Rapid response for sensitive issues and internal investigations

Outcomes matter

Fewer surprises. Cleaner decisions. Consistent practices. A compliance program leadership can defend—and teams can actually run.
