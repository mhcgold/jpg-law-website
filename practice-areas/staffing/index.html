Staffing & Contingent Workforces

Legal for the staffing industry—built from the inside out

Practical legal support for staffing firms, EOR/AOR providers, and companies using contract talent—focused on classification, compliance, and smart risk allocation.

What we do

  • Worker classification analysis and risk assessment (W-2 vs. 1099 vs. employee of record)
  • Staffing agreement drafting and negotiation—MSAs, service agreements, SOWs
  • EOR/AOR agreement review and compliance guidance
  • Joint employer risk analysis and mitigation strategies
  • Multi-state employment compliance for contingent workforce programs
  • Client contract review and markup from the staffing firm's perspective
  • Workforce transition and onboarding legal support
  • Insurance and indemnification structure for staffing arrangements
  • State-specific compliance: wage and hour, benefits, workers' compensation
  • VMS and MSP contract review and negotiation
How clients engage us

Staffing clients typically engage us on a fractional retainer basis for ongoing contract support and compliance guidance, or on a project basis for specific agreement negotiations, audits, or policy work. We work directly with your operations and sales teams—not just the back office.
