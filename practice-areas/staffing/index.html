Staffing & Contingent Workforces

Staffing & Contingent Workforces

Helping staffing providers and contingent workforce programs move fast—without taking on more risk

Staffing and contingent workforce legal support

We advise staffing companies and organizations using contingent labor on the contracts, compliance, and operational guardrails that keep programs scalable. Typical matters include MSAs/SOWs, MSP/VMS terms, EoR/AoR engagement models, worker classification risk, and "bad day" readiness when disputes arise.

Contract Redlines & Negotiation

Tight, business-forward contract support for staffing MSAs, SOWs, rate cards, amendments, and client paper—focused on risk allocation, cashflow terms, and operational reality.

EoR & AoR Engagement Models

Clear "who controls what" contracting for Employer of Record, Agent of Record, and gig programs—scope, responsibilities, onboarding requirements, and protections that match the way the engagement actually runs.

Disputes, Claims, & Bad Days

Indemnity tenders, demand letters, worker/client disputes, insurance coordination, and documentation readiness—so issues are contained early and handled consistently.

Who we support

  • Staffing agencies (IT, healthcare, industrial, professional services)
  • EOR / AOR / PEO providers and platform-enabled workforce companies
  • MSPs, VMS stakeholders, and large program operators
  • Mid-market and enterprise buyers of contingent labor (direct sourcing + staffing mix)
  • Prime contractors and subcontractors supporting government / public-sector work
  • High-growth companies scaling rapidly with a blended employee + contractor workforce
  • Companies expanding into new states with multi-jurisdiction workforce compliance needs

Tools we provide

  • Staffing MSA/SOW clause library + fallback positions (EOR/AOR, IC/1099, conversion, co-employment, indemnity, insurance, background checks, timekeeping)
  • Conversion / non-solicit / non-circumvention riders (placement fees, conversion windows, buyouts, direct-hire, redeployment, and carveouts)

Engagement options

  • Deal Desk (Staffing): Pre-screen, redline, negotiate, and close MSAs/SOWs in-flow—fast and consistent
  • Paper Refresh Sprint (30–45 days): Upgrade templates, playbooks, compliance guardrails, and sales "do/don't" guides
  • Program Launch Support: Stand up a new client/program (VMS/MSP-ready onboarding, pricing mechanics, timekeeping, and operational workflows)
  • On-Call Escalations: Rapid help for red-flag deals, incidents, worker issues, or urgent client demands

Outcomes matter

Faster closes. Cleaner onboarding. Fewer worker issues and client escalations. Agreements that match how staffing actually works—and risk positions leadership can operate.
