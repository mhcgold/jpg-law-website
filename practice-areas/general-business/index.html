Fractional GC support for the legal work that keeps the business moving

General business law support

Most companies don't need a different law firm for every issue—they need one advisor who can spot risk early, keep decisions clean, and move the work to "done." We support leaders across contracting, governance, employment, compliance, and disputes with counsel that is clear, responsive, and aligned to how the business actually operates.

Business Setup & Governance

Entity formation, ownership structure, governance documents, board/consents, and the "corporate housekeeping" that keeps you investable and defensible.

Commercial Contracts

Vendor and customer agreements, MSAs/SOWs, licensing, partnerships, and procurement addenda—reviewed and negotiated with a focus on closing cleanly.

Operational Compliance & Risk Controls

Policies, processes, and documentation that reduce exposure—sized to your industry, footprint, and risk tolerance.

Employment Guidance for Employers

Handbooks, onboarding/offboarding, wage/hour basics, manager issues, and support on sensitive employee situations—built to prevent avoidable claims.

Disputes, Escalations, & Settlement

Early strategy, demand letters, negotiation, and resolution documents—so conflict doesn't become a runaway cost center.

Outside Counsel & Matter Management

When litigation or specialty work is needed, we help scope it, control spend, manage communication, and keep decisions and documentation aligned.

Who we support

  • Founder-led and owner-operated businesses that need ongoing legal coverage
  • Growth companies adding vendors, customers, and employees quickly
  • Operators (Sales, Ops, HR, Finance) who want fast answers and usable guidance
  • Teams that need contract support without slowing revenue cycles
  • Leaders who want a single point of coordination across legal issues
  • Businesses operating across multiple states with evolving compliance needs

Tools we provide

  • "First-pass" contract playbook + fallback positions (by deal type)
  • Document templates (basic vendor/customer terms, NDA, SOW, policy starter set)
  • Governance toolkit (consents, delegations of authority, signature controls)
  • HR/people risk pack (handbook modules, manager guides, separation checklists)
  • Issue triage checklists (what matters, what to preserve, who approves)
  • Outside counsel briefing template (facts, objectives, budget guardrails)

Engagement options

  • Fractional General Counsel: Ongoing legal coverage across the business
  • Contract Desk: Review/redline/negotiation support in-flow for active deals
  • Legal Ops Tune-Up (30–45 days): Templates, playbooks, and approval workflows
  • On-Call Escalations: Rapid support for urgent issues, disputes, and people matters

Outcomes matter

Faster decisions. Cleaner contracts. Fewer surprises. Legal guidance that teams can use—without slowing the business down.
