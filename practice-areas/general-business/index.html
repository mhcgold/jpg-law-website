How clients engage us

General business clients often start with a specific contract or issue and grow into an ongoing fractional relationship as they realize how much legal work is quietly piling up. We're particularly useful for companies that don't have in-house counsel but deal with a steady stream of contracts, employee questions, and operational decisions with legal dimensions.
