Jason Paul Gehlhausen is a business attorney and fractional general counsel with over 15 years of practice spanning employment law, technology transactions, privacy and data protection, commercial contracts, and staffing industry legal services.

Jason founded JPG Law to give growth-stage companies and operationally complex businesses access to the kind of strategic, in-house legal perspective that's typically out of reach—without the overhead of a full-time hire. His practice is built on the belief that the best legal counsel is proactive, integrated into business operations, and delivered with the same urgency and context that your leadership team brings to every decision.

His work focuses on the legal issues that sit at the intersection of people, contracts, data, and risk—areas that matter for companies growing fast, building distributed teams, or managing complex vendor and client relationships. He has particular depth in staffing and contingent workforce law, helping staffing firms, EOR/AOR providers, and their clients navigate classification risk, compliance obligations, and smart contract structures.

Jason holds a CIPP/US certification from the International Association of Privacy Professionals, reflecting his work on privacy program development, CCPA/GDPR compliance, and data governance for technology and data-intensive businesses. He has advised employers across all 50 states on multi-state employment compliance, helped companies structure and negotiate SaaS and vendor agreements, and guided founders through entity formation, financing rounds, and early-stage operational setup.

Before launching JPG Law, Jason worked in private practice and in-house environments where he developed a reputation for translating complex legal obligations into practical, business-first strategies. He is licensed to practice law in Indiana and serves clients across the country.
