Practical Legal Counsel That Scales With Your Business

JPG Law is a boutique business law practice built on one operating principle: legal should work for your business, not the other way around.

What We Do

Fractional general counsel — the practical in-house alternative

Fractional legal support gives you experienced, strategic counsel on a part-time or project basis—so you get the benefits of in-house leadership without the cost or commitment of a full-time hire.

It's a practical fit for startups and growth-stage companies, as well as established teams running lean, because it bridges the gap between one-off legal tasks and proactive, integrated legal guidance tied to how your business actually operates.

JPG Law provides flexible, business-forward support that scales with your needs—whether that's day-to-day issue spotting, a financing or expansion push, or ongoing contract and compliance coverage.
