About JPG Law

Advising clients globally, for more than 15 years

For more than 15 years, JPG Law has advised founders, employers, and leadership teams on the legal issues that sit at the center of operations: people, contracts, data, and risk. We serve as fractional general counsel and strategic deal counsel, helping clients make confident decisions, move faster, and avoid preventable problems through clear guidance and well-built documentation.

Our work spans multi-state employment and contingent workforce matters, technology and SaaS transactions, privacy and information governance, commercial contracting, and corporate compliance. Clients value a practical approach: direct communication, business-aligned risk allocation, and solutions that are designed to work in the real world, not just on paper.
